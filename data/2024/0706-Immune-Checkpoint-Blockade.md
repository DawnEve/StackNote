

# 巨噬细胞来源的CXCL9和CXCL10是免疫检查点阻断后抗肿瘤免疫应答所必需的(Clin Cancer Res. 2020)
> Macrophage-Derived CXCL9 and CXCL10 Are Required for Antitumor Immune Responses Following Immune Checkpoint Blockade
> https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31636098/
> Cancer Immunology Program, Peter MacCallum Cancer Centre, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia.

Response rates to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB; anti-PD-1/anti-CTLA-4) correlate with the extent of tumor immune infiltrate, but the mechanisms underlying the recruitment of T cells following therapy are poorly characterized. 

The chemokines upregulated by dual PD-1/CTLA-4 blockade were assessed using NanoString-based analysis with results confirmed at the protein level by flow cytometry and cytometric bead array.

Results: 
- The CXCR3 ligands, CXCL9 and CXCL10, were significantly upregulated following dual PD-1/CTLA-4 blockade and both CD8+ T-cell infiltration and therapeutic efficacy were CXCR3 dependent. 双阻断后CXCR3的受体（CXCL9 和 CXCL10）显著上调。T细胞浸润和疗效都是 CXCR3 依赖的。
- In both murine models and patients undergoing immunotherapy, macrophages were the predominant source of CXCL9 and their depletion abrogated CD8+ T-cell infiltration and the therapeutic efficacy of dual ICB. 小鼠模型和免疫治疗的病人中，巨噬细胞是CXCL9的主要来源。删除后，废除了CD8+T细胞浸润，及双阻断疗效。
- Single-cell RNA-seq analysis of patient tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TIL) revealed that CXCL9/10/11 was predominantly expressed by macrophages following ICB and we identified a distinct macrophage signature that was associated with positive responses to ICB. 单细胞分析肿瘤浸润淋巴细胞揭示，双阻断后，CXCL9/10/11 主要来自巨噬细胞，发现响应免疫阻断剂的巨噬细胞基因谱。
